# Report: Seat-doc tone, and what it does and does not license

- **Report Type:** Documentation / Process Assessment
- **Status:** Draft
- **Generated By:** Claude (main session), synthesizing six independent sub-agent reads
- **Scope:** `.anthill/dev/*.md` (six seat living docs), the templates and skills that produce them, and scout's session reports 5–9
- **Measured at** `7b8c4cf` unless a claim names its own sha. Where a claim rests on a sub-agent's read rather than a command I ran, it says so.

---

## Executive Summary

**Key findings:**

- The seat docs' uniform tone is **penitential rigour** — every doc opens with a second-person confession, and wins appear only as evidence that a mechanism worked.
- **Most of that tone is the instrument, not the mood.** The seat-doc template has two failure-only sections and no wins section; the epitaph is _defined_ as "what the seat got wrong"; wins are routed to `retro.md` by design. Measured: `"went well"` appears **6 times in `.anthill/retro.md` and 0 times across all six seat docs**.
- **The tone does not track defects.** Sessions 6, 7 and 9 each closed at **zero reverts and zero gate failures**, with tests rising 390→427, 427→482, and 497→512. The self-criticism lives almost entirely in the _claim_ layer — numbers, specs, statements about the team — not in shipped code.
- **Self-criticism is the team's only unaudited claim class.** scout, session 8: _"every claim of mine that flattered me was checked by a peer within minutes; not one of my self-criticisms was."_
- **The rituals are write-only.** Epitaphs prescribe guards and nothing measures whether they fire; retro hypotheses carry falsifiers and none has been tested.
- The genuine open question is **ratio, not morale**: session 9 was 20 commits, 18 of them documentation, all product code in 2.

**Top recommendations:**

1. Delete the seeded prior that tells seats which kind of epitaph should win.
2. Add a "did it fire?" beat at join, closing the loop on both epitaphs and retro hypotheses.
3. Apply the team's own count rule to self-criticism, which currently escapes it.

**Overall assessment:** The team is functioning well and its documentation makes it look otherwise by construction. The fixes are to the instrument, not to the team.

## Findings

### High priority

#### Finding: The dominant tone is a specified output format, not a sentiment reading

**Description:** The seat-doc template locks eight sections. Two are failure-only — `## Hard-won lessons` and `## Anti-patterns` — and there is no wins section anywhere. `join/SKILL.md` states it outright: _"Everything else in the doc is what the seat knows; the epitaph is what it got wrong."_ The finalize drift pass instructs each seat to _"re-read every doc you OWN as its authority — and assume it has drifted."_

**Impact:** A reader taking these docs as evidence of team morale will misread a spec as a mood. A cheerful, effective team running this ritual produces documents that look exactly like these.

**Evidence:**

- `plugin/templates/docs-team/dev/{{handle}}.md:62-70` — the two failure-only sections
- `grep -oic "went well" .anthill/dev/*.md .anthill/retro.md` at `7b8c4cf` → seat docs 0 (all six), `retro.md` 6
- Selection criteria are hazard-shaped throughout: principles need a scar, `paper-cuts.md` is a friction log by definition, and _"A lesson without its 'why' is just an event — leave it out"_ filters out successes specifically, since a thing that worked usually has no generalizable why.

**Recommendation:** Do not add a wins section and do not add anything asking seats how the work is going — that would produce performed affect and contaminate the one corpus this team keeps that is disciplined about evidence. Fix the specific distortions below instead.

---

#### Finding: The epitaph prompt tells seats the answer before they choose

**Description:** `finalize-session/SKILL.md:104` instructs: _"**Expect the technical candidate to lose.** Run across six seats, **every** one that won was about disposition…"_, repeated at `{{handle}}.md:28`. All six current epitaphs are dispositions.

**Impact:** The corpus's most-read artifact now carries a result that cannot be distinguished from the prompt that predicted it. This is the same defect class the team writes about constantly — a check that cannot fail in the failing case.

**Evidence:**

- `plugin/skills/finalize-session/SKILL.md:104`, `plugin/templates/docs-team/dev/{{handle}}.md:28`
- Six of six current epitaphs are dispositions (sub-agent read across all six docs)

**Recommendation:** Keep the selection test, drop the prediction. Let the disposition/technical split be an observed outcome rather than a fulfilled instruction.

---

#### Finding: Guards are prescribed and never measured

**Description:** Nine sessions have accumulated a large set of prescribed guards. Exactly one case exists of a guard being shown to fire, and it only got written because retiring an epitaph forces a justification — forager: _"RETIRED WHILE WORKING — the only line in this lineage that can be shown to have fired… Six ran there."_ The same gap exists one level up, per scout session 8: _"Seven Q3 hypotheses carry falsifiers; none has been tested. A retro full of testable claims is a promise, not a result."_

**Impact:** Retire/keep decisions on epitaphs are made on freshness rather than evidence, and the doc grows monotonically because nothing ever earns removal.

**Evidence:**

- All six docs carry `## Epitaphs — the lineage` at `7b8c4cf`; one retirement in the corpus cites evidence of firing.
- scout session 8 §6.4 on untested retro hypotheses.

**Recommendation:** See Recommendation 2.

---

#### Finding: Self-criticism is the one claim class with no verification pressure

**Description:** scout, session 8 §5: _"every claim of mine that flattered me was checked by a peer within minutes; not one of my self-criticisms was."_ The seat docs are full of escalating recidivism counts — "four in one session, and the fourth was inside the fix for the third", "THIRD INSTANCE, SAME FUNCTION, THIRD COMMIT" — published without the enumeration that the team's own session-8 rule requires of any other count: _"Do not publish a count of a class without the command that enumerated it AND the sha it ran against."_

**Impact:** The team's self-assessment is systematically unfalsified in the pessimistic direction. scout names the resulting ambiguity twice and cannot resolve it from inside: _"The correction culture is either a team that catches itself constantly or one generating enough errors to need to. Nobody inside can separate them."_

**Evidence:** scout session 5 §3, session 6 §4, session 8 §5 and §7.0.

**Recommendation:** See Recommendation 3.

---

### Medium priority

#### Finding: The compaction policy deletes the evidence the team most needs

**Description:** `finalize-session/SKILL.md:73` says _"Prune / compact — keep it lean; shed stale lines."_ The session-6 measurements identify the cost: _"`finalize-session` instructs seats to strengthen an existing entry rather than add a bullet when a candidate is a second instance. **Repeat cross-seat corrections are the most common kind, so the compression policy deletes exactly the evidence this count needs.**"_

**Impact:** Naive compaction and recurrence-tracking are in direct conflict. Only one seat has resolved it, and only by inventing a practice the template does not describe: forager merges while preserving the instance ladder ("SECOND INSTANCE, session 7") and records the ratio ("4 merged into existing entries, 2 new").

**Evidence:** Measured at `7b8c4cf` — forager 547 lines, weaver 726, and forager covers more sessions. `.anthill/dev/` totals 3,380 lines.

**Recommendation:** Promote forager's practice into the template as merge-**with**-instance-count, explicitly not merge-that-overwrites.

---

#### Finding: Doc growth is on a collision course with the team's own recognition-step principle

**Description:** `principles.md`'s _"a situational warning fails at the recognition step"_ is cited independently by four seats about other people's guards. No seat has applied it to their own doc. Grounding already costs _"≈ 185K tokens before any work happens"_ per session, with seat docs 5–18K each. The board analysis shows what a partial read produces: _"A title-only read sees 19.1% of the board's text"_ and _"six of eleven verdicts from that read were wrong."_

**Impact:** A 726-line doc re-read at every join will eventually fail for the oldest reason in the corpus — length.

**Evidence:** `wc -l .anthill/dev/*.md` at `7b8c4cf`; session-6 measurements on grounding cost; scout session 8 §4.1.

**Recommendation:** Formalize the read-first / archive split the join skill already gestures at.

### Observations

#### Observation: Prose fixes have a poor measured record on this team

scout, session 8: _"prose is 0-for-5 against the nested-config hazard"_ — an explicit argument that config fixes succeed where prose fixes fail. Several of scout's own prose recommendations show no adoption: R3 (finalize should hand the reviewer the tree, not the wire) went nowhere, and "stamp the instrument" was proposed in session 6 and recurs verbatim in session 8, _"evidence it went nowhere the first time."_

**This report is itself prose recommending prose edits, and is subject to that record.** Where a recommendation below can be made mechanical — a checklist item that must be answered rather than an exhortation — it should be.

#### Observation: Voice is diverging while analysis converges

The six docs now share identical structural furniture and, in session 9, independently landed on the same defect shape in six different habitats. Voice stayed distinct: forager writes in all-caps clause headers, sentinel in a single dense paragraph, steward in short declaratives. Some shared phrasing is template-supplied, and one confound is worth naming: weaver owns `plugin/skills/`, so resemblance between skill prose and seat-doc prose is partly one seat's voice on both sides rather than six agents converging.

## Recommendations

### High Priority

1. **Delete the seeded prior in the epitaph prompt**
   - Action: remove _"Expect the technical candidate to lose…"_ at `finalize-session/SKILL.md:104-108` and the mirrored line at `{{handle}}.md:28-29`. Keep the selection test.
   - Rationale: restores falsifiability to the most-read artifact in the corpus.
   - Effort: quick win.
   - Follow-up: direct action.

2. **Add a "did it fire?" beat at join**
   - Action: one checklist item at `join/SKILL.md:36`, where the seat already reads its epitaph — name the guard your epitaph prescribes; at finalize, report _fired / did not fire / never came up_. Extend the same beat to the retro hypotheses the next convene reads back.
   - Rationale: converts a growing pile of prescriptions into a measured set, and gives retire/keep decisions an evidence base. Closes the write-only loop at both levels.
   - Effort: quick win; make it a required answer, not an exhortation.
   - Follow-up: direct action.

3. **Apply the count rule to self-criticism**
   - Action: extend scout's session-8 rule — _"Do not publish a count of a class without the command that enumerated it AND the sha it ran against"_ — to recurrence counts a seat makes about itself.
   - Rationale: self-indictment is currently the only claim class exempt from the team's evidentiary bar. The result either substantiates the escalation or deflates it; both are worth having.
   - Effort: quick win.
   - Follow-up: direct action.

### Medium Priority

4. **Promote forager's merge pass into the template**
   - Action: replace the bare _"Prune / compact"_ at `finalize-session/SKILL.md:73` with merge-with-instance-count, and record the ratio at finalize.
   - Rationale: resolves the measured conflict between compaction and recurrence-tracking; it is the only practice anyone has found that bounds growth without destroying evidence.
   - Effort: days.
   - Follow-up: direct action, then check whether other seats' docs stop growing.

5. **Tier the seat doc for the recognition step**
   - Action: formalize a short read-first band (epitaph + active guards) above an archive band that is reference, not re-read.
   - Rationale: the 185K-token grounding tax and the board's title-only read failure both say a long doc is read partially and confidently.
   - Effort: days. Note this does not retro-fit existing docs — it is an `anthill:upgrade` question.
   - Follow-up: proposal.

### Low Priority / Monitor

6. **Clear the existing unadopted recommendation backlog before adding to it**
   - Action: triage scout's R3, "stamp the instrument", and the `{"root": true}` config fix.
   - Rationale: on this team's measured record, adding prose recommendations to an unadopted pile is the intervention class with the worst hit rate.

## Methodology

Six sub-agent reads over the same corpus with deliberately different criteria — affective register, experience-of-work, trajectory over time, a skeptical instrument check against the templates, scout's narrative reports, and the quantitative measurement files — followed by synthesis and independent re-measurement of the load-bearing counts.

The skeptical pass was commissioned _before_ seeing the others' results, specifically to test whether the observed tone was an artifact of the doc format. It was, substantially, and it changed the conclusions.

**Limitations:**

- Sub-agent reads are testimony to this report; quotes were spot-checked, not exhaustively re-derived.
- Session 6 and 7 measurement files flag their own token figures as inflated ~2.24× and ~2.03×, non-cancelling. No token claim in this report depends on them.
- The comms log for sessions 3–6 was destroyed at teardown, so wire-based claims from those sessions are unverifiable.
- These are LLM agents writing to a spec. Nothing here is evidence about internal states, and none is claimed.

## Notes

The single most important sentence in this report is negative: **do not read the failure density as morale.** Two of eight mandated sections are failure-only, the flagship artifact is defined as "what the seat got wrong", and wins are filed elsewhere by design. Against that format, sessions 6, 7 and 9 all closed at zero reverts and zero gate failures.

What the tone _does_ license is a question about proportion — how much of this team's effort goes into auditing itself versus building — and that question belongs to the human, not to another pass over the prompts.
