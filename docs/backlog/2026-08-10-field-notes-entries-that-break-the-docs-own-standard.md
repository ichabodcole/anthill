# Field-notes entries that break the document's own stated standard

**Added:** 2026-08-10 · **Status:** Open · **Shape:** four entries, audited against the preamble
**Surface:** `plugin/scripts/anthill/field-notes.md` · ships to every team

**Found by a blind cold read, 2026-08-10** — by a reader that had learned the standard _from the
document_ and then applied it back to the document, which is the strongest possible form of this
finding.

## 1. Entries with no scar, against a preamble that promises one

The document opens: _"Each carries the evidence that produced it"_, and states elsewhere that **a
principle without its experience is a slogan.** The reader named four entries carrying no scar:

- "Verify a claim that indicts you as hard as one that flatters you"
- "A count is not a reading"
- "There is no message budget"
- "Prefer the engineering account to the personal one"

> _"I have no way to check whether those were paid for or just believed, and the document taught me
> to ask."_

Each is either given its incident or moved out of a document whose whole claim is that these were
paid for.

## 2. A bare count, two entries below the entry warning against bare counts

`field-notes.md:56`:

> _"In one session, one team hit this eight separate times, across four different people"_

The reader: _"Eight times out of how many checks? Four people out of how many on the team? I can't
tell if that's an epidemic or background noise."_ The document's own rule — and `seams.md`'s ratified
authoring note, adopted after counts rotted three times — is **say what the number meant, not what it
was.** This survived a pass that rewrote all eleven scars for exactly this.

## 3. The two longest entries are the two the reader had to re-read

The guard entry and the cold-read entry. The reader's closing observation:

> _"The entries that landed hardest were the short ones with a concrete, costly, specific incident.
> The two longest are the ones where I had to re-read to keep the thread — and the cold-read entry is
> itself the one warning against exactly that."_

Both were written in the last two days, by the author of this item, immediately after a cold read
scored them as improved. **Improved against their previous selves is not the same as short.**

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Every entry either carries its incident or is moved out
- [ ] `:56`'s count is replaced by what it meant
- [ ] The two long entries are cut to the length of the ones that landed
- [ ] Re-cold-read blind, and compare the clarity ranking rather than asking whether it improved
