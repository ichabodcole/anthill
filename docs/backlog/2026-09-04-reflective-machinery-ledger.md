## The class

A device meant to fire later, such as an epitaph, a hypothesis with a falsifier, a verdict, or an
adopted principle, or the record of one not firing for its author.

four disposition fields filled.

## The intervention shape (converged, both merges)

One ledger with unique ids, a falsifier, an owner and a status, read at convene, where `NO DATA` is a
row rather than an absence. Guards are bound to the act by a hook and scored by the successor session,
so a lesson has a fired or did-not-fire tally instead of a permanent line in a doc.

## Before designing

- Run curation on the migration batch first. The report front matter (`curated: { at, by }`) and the
  routing decision are already a minimal ledger; find out what is missing before adding a file.
- Decide who scores. "Scored by the successor" means the next session's convene reads the ledger and
  reports what fired; that is a convene touchpoint change on the wiki, not a skill change.
- Spellbook's evidence says routing lessons into `seams.md` overgrows it. The ledger must not be a new
  place for the same prose.
