# Join

## What this touch point is

Fires once per seat per session, run by the seat, via `anthill:join <handle>`. The skill prints
the manifest with every path resolved and reads this page.

**What it must read, in order:** the project's grounding docs; `principles.md`; `seams.md` (you
defer to these, you never restate them); your own seat doc — five sections, identity only.
**What it must produce:** your scratch file and **your session report**, both minted empty, and
your presence on the channel and the board.

**What it must NOT do:** edit your seat doc, `seams.md`, `principles.md`, or this wiki. Those are
curated. If any of them reads wrong to you, that is the first line of your report.

## anthill's suggestions

- **Your seat doc is short on purpose.** It says who you are and what you own. It does not say
  what went wrong last time; that was tried, and reading it did not stop the next seat repeating
  it. Ground in the identity and go.
- **Read the ruling after you act, not before — that is structural.** The wire's history replays
  every session; a bare read shows you the past. Take the current state from the manifest and the
  board, and do not infer it from the first message you see.
- **If everything reads thin, you may be on the wrong team.** A wrong team binding looks exactly
  like amnesia. Check which team you are on before concluding the docs are empty.
