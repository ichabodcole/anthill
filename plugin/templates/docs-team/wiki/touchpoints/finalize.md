# Finalize

## What this touch point is

Fires once per session, at the end, via `anthill:finalize-session`. Each seat runs its part; the
lead runs the whole-session part and tears down last.

**What each seat must produce:** a **closed report** — the running capture plus a short close
(see the prompts below). **What the lead must produce:** the lead report, carrying what the next
convene must read; and the docs-of-record sweep. **What nobody produces:** an edit to a seat doc,
`seams.md`, `principles.md`, or this wiki. The skill asserts it — a curated file that changed
during the session is the one thing finalize refuses.

## anthill's suggestions

### Close your report

Three open prompts, answered in prose, as long as they need to be. They ask what happened; they
do not ask you to sort it.

1. **What did you hit?** Everything that cost you time, surprised you, or went wrong — including
   things you fixed. Name the project's tools, docs and teammates freely; your reader has them.
2. **What worked?** The move, tool, or sequence you would do again. This is where playbooks come
   from, and it is the half every retro under-reports.
3. **What would you change — about your seat doc, a seam, a principle, this wiki, or the team's
   shape?** Proposals, not edits. If you had one thing to tell your successor, say it here: it is
   interesting, and curation decides whether it is load-bearing.

### The lead's report

The same three, plus **carry-forward**: what the next session must test, decide, or not do — each
with its falsifier. This is the one report the next convene reads. Keep it to what changes the
next session's first hour.

### The docs-of-record sweep

The docs that claim something true of the tree and that nobody owns — a plan of record, a
roadmap, a proposal — go stale at the speed the work moves. **The lead names who checks each
one.** Write a dated reconciliation line into each doc, claim by claim: `HELD` / `FALSIFIED
<what> → <where it moved>` / `UNCHECKED`. `UNCHECKED` is a real verdict and must be written; a
claim silently skipped is indistinguishable from one confirmed.

### Then land, then down

Each seat lands its own report. The lead lands what is genuinely cross-seat and stands itself
down last.
